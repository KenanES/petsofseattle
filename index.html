        Seattle pets, by the numbers

        Figures as of November 18, 2026, from active City of Seattle pet licenses (newest license in the data: October 6, 2026).

        What is the most popular pet name in Seattle?

        Luna. 358 licensed pets in Seattle are named Luna: 247 dogs and 111 cats. The next most popular names are Lucy (220 pets), Charlie (219), Daisy (180) and Milo (164).

        What is the most popular dog name in Seattle?

        Luna, with 247 licensed dogs, followed by Charlie (176) and Lucy (161).

        What is the most popular cat name in Seattle?

        Luna, with 111 licensed cats, followed by Lucy (59) and Lily (51).

        What is the most common dog breed in Seattle?

        The Labrador Retriever, with 2,273 licensed dogs. Next are the short coat Chihuahua (1,344) and the Golden Retriever (1,276).

        What is the most common cat breed in Seattle?

        The Domestic Shorthair, with 6,972 licensed cats, more than half of all licensed cats. Next are the American Shorthair (1,498) and the Domestic Medium Hair (1,440).

        How many pets are licensed in Seattle?

        38,413 pets have an active City of Seattle license: 25,617 dogs, 12,782 cats, 11 goats and 3 pigs. That's about two dogs for every cat.

        Which Seattle neighborhood has the most pets?

        ZIP code 98115 (Wedgwood, Ravenna and View Ridge) has the most licensed pets, 3,500. Next are 98103 (Fremont, Wallingford and Green Lake) with 3,138 and 98117 (Crown Hill and North Ballard) with 2,555.

        Where does this data come from?

        From the City of Seattle's public Seattle Pet Licenses dataset, which lists every pet with an active license. Pets of Seattle loads it live on each visit. It only includes licensed pets, so the true number of pets in Seattle is higher.
